Pro subscription

  • You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment.
  • You can cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep Pro features until the end of the paid period.
  • Renewal payments are refundable within 14 days if you have not used Pro features in the new period.

Launch (deployment service)

  • Full refund any time before we start work on your project.
  • If we cannot deliver a working deployment, you get a full refund.
  • After a working deployment is delivered, the service is not refundable.

How to request a refund

Email hi@vibe-show.com with the email used at checkout and your order number. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected by this policy.
