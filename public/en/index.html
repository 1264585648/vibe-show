How it works

  1. Pick a prefix. Choose 3–30 lowercase letters, digits or hyphens, e.g. tarot.
  2. Send us your project. Share the prefix, project name and the URL where it's hosted.
  3. We review it. Usually within 24 hours (2 hours on Pro). We check that the site loads and follows our rules.
  4. It goes live. Your subdomain gets a proxied DNS record and HTTPS, and your project joins the showcase.

FAQ

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Free subdomains stay free for as long as your project is reachable and follows our rules.

Do I need to configure DNS on my host?

No. Our Cloudflare edge proxy serves your existing deployment at your subdomain. You only send us the project URL.

Which hosts are supported?

Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Lovable, Bolt, Replit and any public HTTPS site.

What isn't allowed?

Phishing, gambling, adult content, malware, spam and anything that infringes others' rights. See the Terms of Service.

How do I cancel or delete my data?

Email hi@vibe-show.com. See the Privacy Policy and Refund Policy.
