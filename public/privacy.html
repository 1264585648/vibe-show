English

What we collect

  • Project details you submit: subdomain prefix, project name, source URL, author nickname and contact email.
  • Technical data: IP address and a random browser token, used only to rate-limit subdomain reservations and prevent abuse; standard request logs kept by our hosting provider, Cloudflare.
  • Billing data (paid plans): handled by our payment provider. We receive your name, email, country and transaction status, never your full card number.
  • Deployment service: repository access you grant us, used only to deploy your project and removed after delivery.

How we use it

To review and run your subdomain, show your project in the showcase (name, description, link and screenshot only), send service notices about review results and site status, process payments, and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data or use your email for advertising.

Third parties

Cloudflare (hosting, DNS, edge proxy), our payment provider (billing), and Google Fonts (web fonts loaded by your browser). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Retention

We keep project data while your subdomain is active and delete it within 30 days after the subdomain is revoked or you ask us to. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or hide your showcase card, by emailing hi@vibe-show.com. We reply within 30 days.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date above.

中文

我们收集的信息

  • 你提交的作品信息：子域名前缀、作品名称、源站链接、作者昵称和联系邮箱。
  • 技术数据：IP 地址和随机生成的浏览器标识，仅用于子域名锁号限流和防滥用；以及托管服务商 Cloudflare 保存的常规访问日志。
  • 付费信息（付费方案）：由支付服务商处理。我们只会收到姓名、邮箱、国家和交易状态，不会接触完整卡号。
  • 代部署服务：你授权给我们的代码仓库访问权限，仅用于部署你的作品，交付后即解除。

信息用途

用于审核和运行你的子域名、在秀场展示作品（仅名称、简介、链接和截图）、发送审核结果和站点状态等服务通知、处理付款以及防止滥用。我们不会出售你的数据，也不会把邮箱用于广告营销。

第三方服务

Cloudflare（托管、DNS、边缘反向代理）、支付服务商（计费）和 Google Fonts（浏览器加载网页字体）。它们各自按照自己的隐私政策处理数据。

保存期限

子域名有效期间保留作品数据；子域名被回收或你要求删除后 30 天内删除。付费记录按税务法规要求的期限保存。

你的权利

你可以发邮件到 hi@vibe-show.com，要求查看、更正、导出或删除你的数据，或者隐藏秀场卡片。我们会在 30 天内答复。

未成年人

本服务不面向 16 周岁以下用户。

政策更新

更新会在本页公布，并修改上方的生效日期。
